# Business Model

> Draft. Prices are **assumptions** until Applink confirms allowed price points and revenue share.

## Revenue streams
| Stream | Payer | Price (draft) | Via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Boi-O Pro** | Readers | ৳30 / month | Applink Subscription |
| **Daily Boi SMS** (quote + streak nudge) | Feature-phone readers | ৳2 / day *or* bundled in Pro | Applink Subscription + SMS |
| **Author tips** | Readers | ৳2 / ৳5 / ৳10, platform keeps 15% | Applink CaaS |
| **Promoted listings / Boi Mela stall boost** | Publishers | ৳1,000–5,000 / campaign | Invoice (B2B) |

**Free forever:** search, library, ratings, reviews, SMS logging, USSD Boi Mela menu. Free features drive growth; Pro adds depth.

**Pro includes:** advanced reading stats, unlimited shelves, Boi Mela wishlist + budget, ad-free, Pro badge.

## Why carrier billing
No card or wallet needed. Students and feature-phone users can pay from mobile balance.

## Go-to-market
1. **University reading clubs** (DU, BUET, JU, NSU…): campus ambassadors, club challenges.
2. **Ekushey Boi Mela (Feb 2027):** USSD code on stall posters and bookmarks; publisher partners.
3. **Bookstagram / BookTok BD creators:** quote cards shared to Facebook groups.
4. **Bangla book Facebook groups:** the largest existing communities.
